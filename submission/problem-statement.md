# Problem Statement

Competitive games need fairness, but many anti-cheat systems rely on invasive monitoring or full central trust.

Players may be asked to reveal too much: device data, gameplay telemetry, behavior logs, account links, or private match information. In hidden-state games, full transparency also breaks the game because private cards, maps, moves, or strategies become visible.

FairProof addresses this gap by separating verification from exposure.

The goal is to let a player prove that a move was valid while revealing only the selected move and a proof receipt. The full hand, salt, opponent state, and future strategy stay private.
